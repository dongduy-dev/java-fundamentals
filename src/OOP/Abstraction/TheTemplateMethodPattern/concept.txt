1. The Template Method Pattern
Think of this as a "Recipe with Optional Steps." In an abstract class, you define a fixed sequence of events (the template),
but you leave the specific details of some steps to the subclasses.
* How it Works:
    - The Template: You create a final method in the abstract class. Being final means subclasses cannot change
    the order of the steps.
    - The Steps: The template method calls other methods. Some of these are abstract (required for children to fill in),
    and some are hook methods (optional regular methods).
* Real-World Example: Data Exporter
